What we do

A real wash, not a rinse

Curbside pickup empties your cart but leaves everything that leaked out of the bag. We clean the part of the cart that actually causes the smell.

Inside and outside

We wash the interior walls and floor where spills collect, then clean the exterior, the lid, and the handle you touch every week.

Odor and residue

High pressure water and biodegradable cleaner break down the film that builds up between pickups. That film is what you smell when you open the lid.

Curbside and done

Leave your empty carts out on your service day. You do not need to be home, and we set them back where we found them.

Why it matters

What builds up in a Texas trash cart

Garbage bags leak. Meat trays, yard waste, diapers and drink cups all drain to the bottom of the cart, and on the Gulf Coast that residue sits in the heat for a full week between pickups.

  • Odor that carries into the garage, driveway and front porch
  • Flies and maggots in the warm months
  • Roaches, ants and rodents drawn to the side of the house
  • Bacteria on the lid and handle your family touches every week
  • Stains and buildup that shorten the life of the cart

Rinsing the outside with a garden hose moves the problem around. Washing the cart out removes the residue so the smell goes with it.

Questions

Answers before your first cleaning

What is included in a bin cleaning?

We wash the interior walls and floor of the cart, the exterior, the lid, and the handle. The goal is to remove the residue left behind by bagged trash, not just to rinse the outside.

How much does it cost?

Two carts cleaned is $25. Pricing for extra carts and for monthly or quarterly service depends on cart count and frequency, so we quote it when you call or text.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave your empty carts at the curb on your scheduled day. Most of our customers are at work when we come through.

How do I prepare my carts?

Have them emptied by your normal trash pickup and leave them at the curb where the truck collects them. We confirm your service day ahead of time.

How often should carts be cleaned?

Along the Gulf Coast most households do well with monthly service through the summer and quarterly in the cooler months. Heat is what turns leftover residue into odor.

What products do you use?

Eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaners along with high pressure water. They are safe around children, pets and lawns.

Do you serve my neighborhood?

We run routes through Arcola, Missouri City, Manvel, Rosharon and Fresno. Check the service area list above, and if your subdivision is not there, call and we will tell you when we are next in the area.

Do you require a contract?

No. One time cleanings are welcome, and recurring service can be paused or stopped whenever you want.
