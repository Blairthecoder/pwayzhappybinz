Why It Matters

What Builds Up in a Texas Trash Bin

Garbage bags leak. Meat trays, yard waste, diapers and drink cups all drain to the bottom of the bin, and on the Gulf Coast that residue sits in the heat for a full week between pickups.

Rinsing the outside with a garden hose just moves the problem around. Washing the bin out removes the residue, so the smell goes with it.
  • Washes grime and residue from the inside, outside, lid and handle
  • Helps remove the residue that contributes to unpleasant bin odors
  • Regular cleaning removes food residue that can attract flies and other pests
  • High pressure washing reaches areas that are difficult to clean with a garden hose
  • Cleaning products are used as part of the washing process
  • Fast, easy and hassle free, you do not lift a finger

Answers Before Your First Cleaning

What Is Included in a Bin Cleaning?

We wash the interior walls and floor of the bin, the exterior, the lid, and the handle. The goal is to remove the residue left behind by bagged trash, not just to rinse the outside.

How Much Does It Cost?

Right now we are running a special of $25 for two bins. Regular one time pricing is $35 for the first bin and $10 for each additional bin. Monthly and quarterly pricing depends on how many bins you have, so we quote it when you call or text.

Do I Need to Be Home?

No. Leave your empty bins at the curb on your scheduled day. Most of our customers are at work when we come through.

How Do I Prepare My Bins?

Have them emptied by your normal trash pickup and leave them at the curb where the truck collects them. We confirm your service day ahead of time.

How Often Should Bins Be Cleaned?

Along the Gulf Coast most households do well with monthly service through the summer and quarterly in the cooler months. Heat is what turns leftover residue into odor.

What Products Do You Use?

We use high pressure water and cleaning products as part of our bin washing process. Contact us for current product details and any household-specific questions. Product claims depend on the label and directions for the products being used.

Do You Serve My Neighborhood?

We run routes through Arcola, Missouri City, Manvel, Rosharon and Fresno. Check the service area list above, and if your subdivision is not there, call and we will tell you when we are next in the area.

Do You Require a Contract?

No. One time cleanings are welcome, and recurring service can be paused or stopped whenever you want.
